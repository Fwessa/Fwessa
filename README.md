## ✨ A little about me

Hi, I'm **Luisa T. Dala**, also known as **Fwessa** here on GitHub. I bring together full-stack development, applied AI, and a background in **Multimedia Arts and Animation** to make applications that are useful, understandable, and enjoyable to use.

- 🛠️ **At work:** building and improving internal business systems and workflows as an AI Engineer at Gladex Travel and Tours Corp.
- 🧩 **On the web:** connecting thoughtful interfaces with REST APIs, data, and the details that make a feature work.
- 🎨 **On the creative side:** digital illustration, 3D modeling, UI/UX, and sound.
- 🔎 **In the details:** testing user journeys, tracking down bugs, and making the next iteration better.

> My favorite kind of project? One where a real problem meets a little imagination.
